Hyde Park inventory in 3 weeks: 77 units left the available list, 43 were added (November 2026)

Published: 10 November 2026 · By the Explorer Hyde Park team

Between 19 October 2026 and 10 November 2026, 77 units left the available-for-sale list across Hyde Park’s projects and 43 new units were added, taking availability from 509 to 475 units. This article shows exactly where the movement was: which project, which unit type, and what changed in prices.

In brief

  • Overall: Hyde Park’s projects had 509 units available on 19 October 2026 and 475 on 10 November 2026.
  • Movement: 77 units left the available list and 43 new units were added.
  • Fastest-moving project: One Hyde Park, where 40 units left the list and 20 were added.
  • Where stock grew: Hyde Park Signature (48 to 56).
  • One-bedroom apartments: 12 left the list and none were added, taking the total from 101 to 89.
  • Prices: none of the 432 units that stayed available changed price.

Where these numbers come from

The developer issues a report of the units available for sale, each with its code, price and area. We compared the 19 October 2026 report with the 10 November 2026 one, unit by unit, for the projects on this site.

Note: when a unit disappears from the report, it has left the available list — not necessarily been sold. It may be reserved, or withdrawn by the developer, and the report doesn’t say which. That’s why we say “left the list”, not “sold”.

We compared the price of every unit that appears in both reports: all 432 units that stayed available kept the same price. There was no repricing in these 3 weeks.

What this means if you’re planning to buy

  • Types that are shrinking with nothing new added: twin houses in One Hyde Park (3 remaining), quads in Hyde Park Central (7 remaining), 1-bedroom apartments in One Hyde Park (20 remaining), townhouses in Hyde Park Central (9 remaining), 2-bedroom apartments in Hyde Park New Cairo (15 remaining) and 3-bedroom apartments in Hyde Park Central (17 remaining). If one of these is what you want, the choice of floor, view and size narrows over time.
  • Types that got new stock: 2-bedroom apartments in Hyde Park Signature (11 added), 3-bedroom apartments in Hyde Park Signature (4 added), 3-bedroom garden villas in One Hyde Park (2 added) and 4-bedroom duplexes in Hyde Park Central (2 added). There is more to choose from here than 3 weeks ago.
  • Prices move without an announcement: even with no official increase, when the cheaper units leave, what remains is the more expensive ones. Today’s “from” price is tied to a specific unit that may not be there in a few weeks.
  • This is information, not a recommendation: 3 weeks of movement shows a direction, but the decision depends on your budget and needs — and a unit’s availability and price must be confirmed at the time of booking.

Frequently asked questions

Were the units that left the available list sold?

Not necessarily. The developer’s report lists only units available for sale, so a unit that disappears has left the available list: it may be sold, reserved, or withdrawn by the developer. The report doesn’t say which.

Which Hyde Park project is moving fastest?

Between 19 October 2026 and 10 November 2026 it was One Hyde Park: 40 of its 129 units left the list and 20 new units were added.

Did Hyde Park prices go up in November 2026?

No unit was repriced: all 432 units that stayed available between 19 October 2026 and 10 November 2026 kept the same price. What moved is the starting price of 5 unit types, because the cheapest unit left the list or a cheaper one was added.

Are one-bedroom apartments still available in Hyde Park?

Yes. As of the 10 November 2026 report there are 89 one-bedroom apartments available across Hyde Park’s projects (down from 101), starting from EGP 6,410,000 in Hyde Park Central.

How often are these numbers updated?

With every new availability report from the developer. The figures here compare the 19 October 2026 report with the 10 November 2026 one, and each project page’s prices are refreshed with every report.
