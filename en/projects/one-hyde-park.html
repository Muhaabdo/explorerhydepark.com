About One Hyde Park

One Hyde Park is a gated compound inside Hyde Park New Cairo, launched by Hyde Park Developments in September 2026 on about 240 feddans with around 8,500 homes. It has 6 towers described as the tallest in the 5th Settlement, plus apartments, duplexes, sky villas, garden villas, townhouses, twin houses and standalone villas from 75 to 377 m². Services include two international schools, a main lake for rowing and an internal shuttle, and residents use Ryze Club in Hyde Park New Cairo.

Questions before you decide

What does “Core & Shell” mean?

Core & Shell means the unit is handed over as a concrete structure with finished facades and no interior finishing, so the buyer finishes it to their own taste.

If I pay a bigger down payment, will the installment go down?

Yes. The monthly installment is calculated on the balance after the down payment, so a bigger down payment means a smaller installment. At One Hyde Park the down payment starts from 5% with installments over up to 8 years — try it in the “How much can you pay per month?” calculator on this page.

What’s the difference between One Hyde Park and Hyde Park Central?

One Hyde Park starts from EGP 6,870,000 and offers apartments, duplex, sky villa, garden villa, townhouse, twin house and standalone villa, with 129 units currently available. Hyde Park Central starts from EGP 6,410,000 and offers apartments, townhouse, quad villa, standalone villa and duplex, with 110 units currently available.

Can I buy while living outside Egypt?

Yes. Our team follows up with you online from reservation to contract, and the contract can be signed through a power of attorney.
