About Hyde Park Terraces

Hyde Park Terraces is one of Hyde Park’s newest East Cairo launches for 2026, focused on townhouses and standalone villas designed around open terraces. It covers about 102 feddans at the junction of the Old Sokhna Road and Talaat Harb Road, opposite the German University in Cairo and next to Hyde Park Views, with about 85% of the land given to landscaping and open space.

Questions before you decide

What does “Core & Shell” mean?

Core & Shell means the unit is handed over as a concrete structure with finished facades and no interior finishing, so the buyer finishes it to their own taste.

If I pay a bigger down payment, will the installment go down?

Yes. The monthly installment is calculated on the balance after the down payment, so a bigger down payment means a smaller installment. At Hyde Park Terraces the down payment starts from 10% with installments over up to 8 years — try it in the “How much can you pay per month?” calculator on this page.

What’s the difference between Hyde Park Terraces and Hyde Park New Cairo?

Hyde Park Terraces starts from EGP 23,400,000 and offers townhouse and standalone villa, with 7 units currently available. Hyde Park New Cairo starts from EGP 7,220,000 and offers apartments and standalone villa, with 43 units currently available.

Can I buy while living outside Egypt?

Yes. Our team follows up with you online from reservation to contract, and the contract can be signed through a power of attorney.
