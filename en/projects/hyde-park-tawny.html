Questions before you decide

What does “Core & Shell” mean?

Core & Shell means the unit is handed over as a concrete structure with finished facades and no interior finishing, so the buyer finishes it to their own taste.

If I pay a bigger down payment, will the installment go down?

Yes. The monthly installment is calculated on the balance after the down payment, so a bigger down payment means a smaller installment. At Hyde Park Tawny the down payment starts from 10% with installments over up to 7 years — try it in the “How much can you pay per month?” calculator on this page.

What’s the difference between Hyde Park Tawny and Hyde Park Garden Lakes?

Hyde Park Tawny starts from EGP 15,300,000 and offers townhouse, twin house and standalone villa. Hyde Park Garden Lakes starts from EGP 6,800,000 and offers apartments, townhouse, twin house and standalone villa, with 47 units currently available.

Can I buy while living outside Egypt?

Yes. Our team follows up with you online from reservation to contract, and the contract can be signed through a power of attorney.
