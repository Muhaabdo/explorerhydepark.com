About Hyde Park Garden Lakes

Garden Lakes is one of Hyde Park’s largest projects in West Cairo, designed around man-made lakes and green spaces: about 85% of the land is landscaping and open space, and only 15% is built. Its final phase, Park Residences, is set around a central park and lake.

Questions before you decide

What does “Core & Shell” mean?

Core & Shell means the unit is handed over as a concrete structure with finished facades and no interior finishing, so the buyer finishes it to their own taste.

If I pay a bigger down payment, will the installment go down?

Yes. The monthly installment is calculated on the balance after the down payment, so a bigger down payment means a smaller installment. At Hyde Park Garden Lakes the down payment starts from 10% with installments over up to 8 years — try it in the “How much can you pay per month?” calculator on this page.

What’s the difference between Hyde Park Garden Lakes and Hyde Park Signature?

Hyde Park Garden Lakes starts from EGP 6,800,000 and offers apartments, townhouse, twin house and standalone villa, with 47 units currently available. Hyde Park Signature starts from EGP 6,650,000 and offers apartments, townhouse, twin house and standalone villa, with 48 units currently available.

Can I buy while living outside Egypt?

Yes. Our team follows up with you online from reservation to contract, and the contract can be signed through a power of attorney.
