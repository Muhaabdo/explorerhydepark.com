About Hyde Park Business District

Hyde Park Business District is the office and commercial area of Hyde Park New Cairo, with administrative offices in a range of sizes, including executive triplex offices over three floors. About 70% of the project is green space and landscaping, the buildings sit over an underground car park, and services include a conference centre and a piazza with water features and seating, with shops, cafés and restaurants within walking distance.

Questions before you decide

What does “Core & Shell” mean?

Core & Shell means the unit is handed over as a concrete structure with finished facades and no interior finishing, so the buyer finishes it to their own taste.

If I pay a bigger down payment, will the installment go down?

Yes. The monthly installment is calculated on the balance after the down payment, so a bigger down payment means a smaller installment. At Hyde Park Business District the down payment starts from 5% with installments over up to 8 years — try it in the “How much can you pay per month?” calculator on this page.

Can I buy while living outside Egypt?

Yes. Our team follows up with you online from reservation to contract, and the contract can be signed through a power of attorney.
