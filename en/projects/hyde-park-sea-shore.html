Hyde Park Sea Shore at a glance

Hyde Park Sea Shore is a coastal project by Hyde Park Developments in Ras El Hekma, North Coast. Currently available: 122 units — apartments from 63 to 138 m², standard chalet, townhouse, twin house, twin house with roof, small villa and medium beach villa. Prices start from EGP 9,060,000 with 10% down and installments over up to 6 years, and units are delivered Core & Shell.

Updated: October 2026 · From the developer’s inventory · By the Explorer Hyde Park team

About Hyde Park Sea Shore

Sea Shore is Hyde Park’s first project on the North Coast, in Ras El Hekma, on 240 feddans with investments of about EGP 32.2 billion according to the developer. The resort has more than 25 swimming pools and has launched in phases including Shore Ville, Lagoon Town and Waterside, most recently Shore Residences in 2026.

Questions before you decide

What does “Core & Shell” mean?

Core & Shell means the unit is handed over as a concrete structure with finished facades and no interior finishing, so the buyer finishes it to their own taste.

If I pay a bigger down payment, will the installment go down?

Yes. The monthly installment is calculated on the balance after the down payment, so a bigger down payment means a smaller installment. At Hyde Park Sea Shore the down payment starts from 10% with installments over up to 6 years — try it in the “How much can you pay per month?” calculator on this page.

Can I buy while living outside Egypt?

Yes. Our team follows up with you online from reservation to contract, and the contract can be signed through a power of attorney.

How much do units at Hyde Park Sea Shore cost?

Prices start from EGP 9,060,000 (apartments) and vary by unit type and size.

What payment plan is available?
