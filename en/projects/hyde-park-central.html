About Hyde Park Central

Hyde Park Central is Hyde Park’s fourth project in East Cairo, in the 6th Settlement directly on the Middle Ring Road, one minute from Hyde Park New Cairo. The project covers about 220 feddans, and the master plan leaves around 85% of the land as open and green space. In May 2026 the developer launched the Water Residences phase on 37 feddans inside the project.

Questions before you decide

What does “Core & Shell” mean?

Core & Shell means the unit is handed over as a concrete structure with finished facades and no interior finishing, so the buyer finishes it to their own taste.

If I pay a bigger down payment, will the installment go down?

Yes. The monthly installment is calculated on the balance after the down payment, so a bigger down payment means a smaller installment. At Hyde Park Central the down payment starts from 5% with installments over up to 8 years — try it in the “How much can you pay per month?” calculator on this page.

What’s the difference between Hyde Park Central and Hyde Park New Cairo?

Hyde Park Central starts from EGP 6,430,000 and offers apartments, townhouse, quad villa and standalone villa, with 110 units currently available. Hyde Park New Cairo starts from EGP 7,220,000 and offers apartments and standalone villa, with 43 units currently available.

Can I buy while living outside Egypt?

Yes. Our team follows up with you online from reservation to contract, and the contract can be signed through a power of attorney.
